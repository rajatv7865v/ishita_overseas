Welcome to Ishtha Overseas

A Government of India–aligned overseas recruitment desk, built for hotels, sites and facilities abroad.

Ishtha Overseas is an India-based recruitment and manpower company focused on placing qualified Indian professionals with employers overseas. We work in the manner expected of a Ministry of External Affairs / Protector General of Emigrants recruitment desk — licensed process, written contracts, and no informal side payments.

From Khatima, Uttarakhand we source chefs, hotel and restaurant teams, construction trades, nurses, technicians and facility staff from across India, then handle screening, documentation and deployment into the Gulf, the wider Middle East, and selected corridors in Africa and Europe.

Consultants specialise by sector, not by volume, so a shortlist matches the employer's actual site, wage structure and contract length — the same standard luxury hotel groups and EPC contractors expect from a long-term recruitment partner.

How a placement moves

From registration to deployment, five stages, tracked start to finish.

Candidates and employers can check status at any stage — nothing sits unexplained in a queue.

01

Register

Candidate submits trade documents and work history; employer shares the role and site brief.

02

Screening

Trade tests, document verification and shortlist review against the role's actual requirements.

03

Interview

Employer interviews shortlisted candidates directly, in person or by video.

04

Documentation

Offer letter, contract, medical clearance and visa paperwork filed and tracked.

05

Deployment

Pre-departure briefing, travel arrangements, and a contact on file once the candidate arrives.

Questions, answered

What employers and candidates usually ask first.

How long does a typical deployment take?+

Once a shortlist is accepted, documentation and visa timelines depend on the destination. Gulf corridors commonly move in a matter of weeks when medicals and contracts are complete. We share a stage-by-stage calendar at the start of every mandate.

Do you charge candidates a placement fee?+

We work within India's overseas recruitment rules. Any permitted costs are explained in writing before a candidate proceeds — there are no informal side payments at the desk.

Can you mobilise in bulk for a project ramp-up?+

Yes. Construction, facilities and hospitality mandates regularly include staggered batches. Screening, interviews and ticketing are scheduled against your site access dates rather than a single dump of CVs.

Which documents should a candidate keep ready?+

A valid passport, trade certificates, recent photographs, experience letters and, where relevant, prior Gulf visas or medical reports. Our consultants will list destination-specific extras after registration.
